# Roadmap

ferrosheet opens a spreadsheet in under a second, lets you find and copy what you need and
make small edits, and saves without surprises. A viewer first, not a full office suite. What
it can do compared with LibreOffice Calc is in the [feature parity](feature-parity.md) table.

## Next

Open work lives in the [issues](https://github.com/10eputzen/ferrosheet/issues). Roughly in
this order:

1. Close the gaps in editing: `#REF!` after undoing a deletion
   ([#3](https://github.com/10eputzen/ferrosheet/issues/3)), column widths that do not move
   ([#4](https://github.com/10eputzen/ferrosheet/issues/4)), paste with formatting
   ([#5](https://github.com/10eputzen/ferrosheet/issues/5)).
2. Save an xlsx without losing comments, validation, filters, tables or charts
   ([#6](https://github.com/10eputzen/ferrosheet/issues/6)).
3. Linux and macOS packages ([#7](https://github.com/10eputzen/ferrosheet/issues/7)).
4. Named ranges ([#8](https://github.com/10eputzen/ferrosheet/issues/8)), print and PDF
   ([#9](https://github.com/10eputzen/ferrosheet/issues/9)), auto-update settings
   ([#11](https://github.com/10eputzen/ferrosheet/issues/11)).

What has shipped is in the [release notes](https://github.com/10eputzen/ferrosheet/releases).
